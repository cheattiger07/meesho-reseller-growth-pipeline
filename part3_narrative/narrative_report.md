# May Ethnic Wear

**Context:** The monthly revenue of Ethnic Wear is compared for April and May 2026, with the revenue measured in INR.

**Insight — Fact:** Revenue rose from ₹104,520.77 in April to ₹185,107.61 in May, which represents a monthly increase of 77.1%, and the number of orders also went up from 64 to 104.

**Implication — Hypothesis:** The rise could have been due to a promotion in May or increased festive demand. The regional manager should look into the promotions that took place in May and compare the times of those promotions with the rise in orders.
